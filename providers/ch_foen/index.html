    Documentation index · Usage
    Provider ch_foen
    Country Switzerland
    Published by Federal Office for the Environment (FOEN / BAFU) and partner measurement networks
    Read from Official BAFU GraphQL API
    Quantities Discharge, stage, water temperature
    Stations in the catalogue 1,341. Availability depends on station, quantity, product and period
    Station metadata Station name, water-body name, drainage area and elevation. See Fields by provider
    Credentials None
    Terms Commercial and non-commercial use permitted; source attribution required

    Retrieve published daily mean discharge at station 2018, selecting the archive variant TagMittel:

    import rivretrieve as rr

selection = rr.find(
    provider="ch_foen", station="2018", quantity="discharge",
    frequency="daily", statistic="mean",
)
daily = rr.pick(selection, variant="TagMittel")
result = rr.fetch(daily, start="2024-01-01", end="2024-01-07", cache="bypass")

preview = result.data.select("time", "time_zone", "value", "unit").head(3)
print(preview.write_csv(float_precision=3), end="")

print(result.data.height)
print([(issue.severity, issue.code) for issue in result.issues])

    Output:

    time,time_zone,value,unit
2023-12-31T23:00:00.000000,+00:00,113.624,m3/s
2024-01-01T23:00:00.000000,+00:00,107.088,m3/s
2024-01-02T23:00:00.000000,+00:00,114.261,m3/s
7
[('info', 'provenance.citation_not_established')]

    The request returned seven daily means in m³/s. The preview shows the first three, rounded to three decimal places. Each day begins at midnight in fixed UTC+1, so 1 January's mean is labelled 31 December at 23:00 UTC. Bare dates select the whole first and last aggregate day. RivRetrieve preserves these UTC labels rather than moving them to midnight UTC or applying summer time.

    The informational issue provenance.citation_not_established means the packaged provider catalogue has no configured citation. It does not mean retrieval failed or that attribution is optional. Use the attribution in Terms. cache="bypass" requests the source rather than a local cache. Values can change; a later retrieval need not reproduce this output exactly. See Usage for selection options and working with returned data.

    Who measures, and who publishes

    The Federal Office for the Environment runs Switzerland's national hydrological monitoring network. BAFU is its German abbreviation, from Bundesamt für Umwelt; FOEN is its English abbreviation. The official data platform brings together BAFU and partner measurement networks. Its publisher name does not mean that BAFU independently measured every observation.

    No API key or account is required to retrieve observations. RivRetrieve searches the station and product catalogue included with the package without contacting BAFU. Fetching observations then requests data from the official API.

    What you can retrieve

    Choose an archive product according to the period and statistic needed for the analysis. For example, the daily mean describes a day as a whole, while the daily maximum describes its highest short-term measurement. The available product types are listed below for discharge (discharge), water level (stage) and water temperature (temperature). A particular station may supply only some of these products.

    Frequency filter Statistic filter Product
    10min mean Ten-minute mean
    hourly mean Hourly mean
    daily mean, min, max Daily mean, minimum or maximum
    monthly mean, min, max Monthly mean, minimum or maximum
    yearly mean, min, max Yearly mean, minimum or maximum

    The API also supplies recent raw readings separately from the archive products above. RivRetrieve has not established the exact period represented by each recent reading or its statistic, such as a mean. Its timestamp marks measurement end, but the averaging duration remains unknown; do not treat the reading as an instantaneous measurement. A daily-mean selection therefore excludes these recent readings and selects only products known to represent daily means.

    For minimum and maximum products, the aggregate period tells you where to look for the extreme, not how long the underlying measurement lasted. A monthly maximum, for example, is the maximum of short-term measurements within the month, rather than the largest daily mean. The exact measurement duration and the time when that maximum occurred remain unknown.

    RivRetrieve retrieves the products BAFU publishes. It does not calculate missing products from other values or combine recent readings with archive aggregates. The public product page's abbreviated list omits some supported monthly and yearly extrema. Availability still varies by station and quantity.

    Quantity filter Source units Returned unit
    discharge m³/s or l/s m³/s (m3/s)
    stage m or cm, with source reference labels where published m (m)
    temperature °C °C (degC)

    RivRetrieve converts litres per second to cubic metres per second and centimetres to metres. For archive data, RivRetrieve reads the unit supplied with the API response. For recent readings, it uses the unit shown for that parameter on the official station page. The original unit remains visible in source_unit; do not assume archive and recent units are identical. Water-level references need care when comparing records. A label such as “above sea level” tells you what the height refers to, but it does not establish which named height reference system (vertical datum) applies to that series. Although station metadata can identify LN02 for the station altitude, that describes the station's elevation, not necessarily the reference used for its water-level observations.

    Series selection

    A station can publish more than one series for the same quantity and product. The archive distinguishes them by a name in its tsName field, which RivRetrieve returns as variant. At station 2602, for example, both TagMittel and TagMittel_01 are daily-mean series covering overlapping periods. The suffix does not tell you which record is better. RivRetrieve keeps the series separate rather than choosing one automatically or combining their values. Selecting a variant explicitly chooses that series. The recent feed is separate and has no archive tsName.

    Inspect the candidates and station metadata before choosing a series:

    candidates = rr.series(selection)
metadata = rr.metadata(selection)

    candidates describes known series, including their units, frequency and statistic; metadata describes the station. The first example explicitly selects TagMittel. Without a specific variant, RivRetrieve may request data again to look for other source series. Selecting a known variant allows it to reuse cached observations for periods already fetched. See Usage for cache policies.

    Recent and older values

    The catalogue lists 1,341 stations, but it does not list every product available at every station. A station being listed does not guarantee data for every product or period. Conversely, a product missing from a station's catalogue entry may still exist at the source. Station operating dates and the timestamps of a few retrieved values do not establish how far the API record extends.

    It remains unclear how far back recent readings can be requested. The API documentation says seven days in one place and twelve hours in another, so RivRetrieve cannot promise either period. A cache can preserve readings already fetched, but it cannot recover readings that disappeared from the source before they were fetched. We are in touch with BAFU and will update this information as soon as these limits are clarified.

    When inspecting a result, distinguish a row whose value is null from a timestamp that has no row at all. In the first case the source returned an entry without a value; in the second there is no returned entry. RivRetrieve preserves that difference and does not fill gaps. A failed request is a third case: inspect result.issues rather than treating a retrieval failure as evidence that the source has no data. Even a successful request only shows what was returned for that station, product and period; it does not prove an uninterrupted record.

    The separate official Hydrological Data Service for watercourses and lakes offers historical data by order, including data beyond what has been verified through this provider. Orders go directly to that service; RivRetrieve does not place them or use the service when an API request fails. The service's statement about 40 days online refers to a different means of obtaining data and does not resolve the conflicting limits for recent GraphQL readings.

    Data status

    BAFU publishes a release and validation status alongside its observations. RivRetrieve keeps the default observation table in result.data unchanged and preserves those source status fields as observation annotations:

    Route group Annotation schema Field Type
    Archive (10min, hourly, daily, monthly, yearly) ch_foen.archive releaseState String
    Recent (discharge_recent, stage_recent, water_temperature_recent) ch_foen.recent releaseStatus Int64

    Inspect available annotation fields and join them onto the observations fetched at the start of this page:

    print(
    rr.annotations(result)
    .select("schema", "name", "dtype", "row_count")
    .write_csv(),
    end="",
)

annotated = result.to_polars(annotations=True).select(
    "time", "value", "unit", "releaseState"
)
print(annotated.head(3).write_csv(float_precision=3), end="")

    Output:

    schema,name,dtype,row_count
ch_foen.archive,releaseState,String,7
time,value,unit,releaseState
2023-12-31T23:00:00.000000,113.624,m3/s,3
2024-01-01T23:00:00.000000,107.088,m3/s,3
2024-01-02T23:00:00.000000,114.261,m3/s,3

    Every daily value in that week carries archive releaseState '3'. Call rr.annotations(result, schema="ch_foen.archive") or rr.annotations(result, schema="ch_foen.recent") to inspect one route schema on its own. Keeping archive and recent tables separate prevents a missing recent or archive column in a combined query from looking like a published null status.

    Source status Publisher meaning
    null or 0 Raw
    1 Unreleased / prechecked
    2 Released / checked
    3 Released / definitive

    BAFU explains that a null status has the same meaning as 0, raw. RivRetrieve keeps explicit null, string '0', and integer 0 as published rather than converting one into another. Published observations whose value is null still retain their status annotations.

    BAFU's German documentation also writes ersetzt (replaced) beside status 3, without defining which series replaced another. RivRetrieve therefore uses these fields only to report source status: it does not filter observations by status, convert codes into a cross-provider quality score, or rank tsName variants.

    Status annotations travel with local cache entries, manual cache="refresh" updates, UTC conversion, subsetting, and exported bundles. See Source annotations for general options, or Receipts when you need the original HTTP response payload.

    Time

    Two time conventions matter when interpreting archive values: the clock used to display the timestamp and the calendar used to define the aggregate period. RivRetrieve returns timestamps in UTC, indicated by time_zone="+00:00". Read this column together with time, whose datetime values do not themselves carry a time zone.

    Ordinary aggregate timestamps identify the start of the represented period. For daily, monthly and yearly products, that period begins at midnight in fixed UTC+1, including summer. Midnight in this calendar is 23:00 UTC on the preceding date, which explains the labels in the retrieval example. February 2024, for instance, runs from 31 January at 23:00 UTC up to, but not including, 29 February at 23:00 UTC. Its January UTC label still represents the February aggregate; changing the label to midnight UTC would change the period it identifies.

    Recent timestamps mark measurement end; their spacing does not establish an averaging duration. For station 7008's unusual MonatsMittel_Orbe_flex series, RivRetrieve has not established the period each value represents, its statistic, or whether its timestamp marks the beginning or end of that period. Neither the series name nor the API route establishes daily or monthly values. The series is therefore excluded by filters requiring those known properties.

    Terms

    The API terms permit commercial and non-commercial use and require attribution containing the author, dataset title and dataset link. The linked open-data terms describe this condition as “Open use. Must provide the source”. These are the platform's terms of use, rather than a CC-BY licence. Attribution is required when using data retrieved through this API.

    Suggested attribution:

    Bundesamt für Umwelt BAFU, Hydrologische Beobachtungen, https://data.bafu.admin.ch/dataproduct-water-observations

    This is a suggested composition of the required fields, not an official template or quotation. An access date is useful but is not one of those three required fields.

    The API allows data requests without an API key or account, but it limits how often a client can request data. Its rate limit is 500 requests per client IP within any rolling five-minute window. Requests above that limit are rejected with HTTP 403, so a large retrieval can fail even though access is open. RivRetrieve does not promise to wait and retry that response automatically. The API also documents pagination and result limits.
