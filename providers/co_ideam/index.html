    Retrieve one week of daily mean discharge at station 0011017010, Aguasal on the Andágueda river in Chocó:

    import rivretrieve as rr

selection = rr.find(
    provider="co_ideam",
    station="0011017010",
    quantity="discharge",
    frequency="daily",
    statistic="mean",
)

result = rr.fetch(selection, start="2010-01-01", end="2010-01-07", cache="bypass")

preview = result.data.select("time", "time_zone", "value", "unit").head(3)
print(preview.write_csv(), end="")

print(result.data.height)
print(result.issues)

    Output:

    time,time_zone,value,unit
2010-01-01T00:00:00.000000,unknown,128.7,m3/s
2010-01-02T00:00:00.000000,unknown,126.3,m3/s
2010-01-03T00:00:00.000000,unknown,162.8,m3/s
7
()

    The request returned seven published daily means in m³/s from IDEAM's Q_MEDIA_D historical CSV file. The preview shows the first three days. time_zone is unknown because the exported CSV files carry no UTC offset and reflect a 1992-1993 national clock change; see Time and data status. The empty tuple () means this retrieval reported no transport or structural issues. Attribution and ShareAlike conditions apply when reusing the data; see Terms and citation.

    cache="bypass", the default for live rr.fetch calls, requests IDEAM's S3 archive directly without reading or writing a local cache. Passing cache="reuse" stores the downloaded series locally so later historical queries within the cached span reuse the local store without downloading the multi-decade CSV file again.     Who measures, and who publishes

    Colombia's Instituto de Hidrología, Meteorología y Estudios Ambientales (IDEAM), a public scientific institute affiliated with the Ministry of Environment and Sustainable Development (Ministerio de Ambiente y Desarrollo Sostenible), operates the national hydrometeorological monitoring network and publishes historical surface-water series from its AQUARIUS Time-Series database to its Open Data S3 portal (datos.ideam.gov.co). Some stations in the national registry are operated by regional environmental authorities (Corporaciones Autónomas Regionales) or hydropower and water utilities and shared through IDEAM's national catalogue.

    No API key or account is required. RivRetrieve searches the packaged station and series catalogue offline; rr.fetch then downloads the corresponding per-station CSV files over HTTPS. Station identifiers are ten-digit zero-padded IDEAM codes, kept as strings such as "0011017010". Include the two leading zeros when selecting by station code, even though station display names often show the eight-digit form in brackets (for example AGUASAL [11017010]).

    The packaged co_ideam catalogue retains 1,372 flowing-watercourse gauges that publish at least one river discharge or stage file in IDEAM's S3 historical archive:

    • Excluded non-river and inactive entries: Socrata station-registry rows with no S3 discharge or stage files, meteorological-only stations, and standing-water stage gauges on reservoirs (Embalse) and lagoons (Laguna) without discharge series are excluded. Gauges classified under a hydrological station category that publish river discharge or stream-named stage records are retained.
    • Separation from legacy HIS_* Socrata tables: Colombia's national open data portal (datos.gov.co) also hosts older tabular datasets named HIS_CAUDAL_D, HIS_NIVEL_D, and similar HIS_* views. Those legacy tables stop around 2019 to 2022 and are superseded by IDEAM's daily-regenerated S3 historical CSV archive (s3-estacionesideam), which carries both the earlier multi-decade history and current operational years. RivRetrieve reads only the S3 historical CSV archive and does not query or merge the legacy HIS_* tables.

    What you can retrieve

    IDEAM publishes up to twenty hydrological CSV series per river gauge: ten for discharge (caudal) and ten for water level (nivel):

    A station may publish only a subset of these twenty files, and a listed series need not cover the requested period. RivRetrieve reads the aggregated daily, monthly, and yearly series directly as IDEAM publishes them; it does not compute daily, monthly, or yearly summaries from sub-daily readings.

    IDEAM's variables.csv dictionary labels CAUDAL_H and NIVEL_H as Horario (hourly) with no statistic code. In practice, those two labels combine hourly telemetry at automatic gauges with twice-daily (06:00 and 18:00) manual staff-gauge readings at conventional gauges, and individual stations switch cadence when upgraded from conventional observation to telemetry. Because one series label spans both observation schedules without declaring whether readings are instantaneous or interval averages, RivRetrieve leaves both frequency and statistic unknown (None) on CAUDAL_H and NIVEL_H.

    Filtering by frequency ("daily", "monthly", or "yearly") and statistic ("mean", "max", or "min") selects the corresponding derived series directly. To inspect all available series at a gauge offline or select CAUDAL_H or NIVEL_H explicitly, inspect rr.series(...) and pick by series_id:

    import polars as pl

all_discharge = rr.find(provider="co_ideam", station="0011017010", quantity="discharge")
candidates = rr.series(all_discharge)
metadata = rr.metadata(all_discharge)

caudal_h_id = candidates.filter(pl.col("published_id") == "CAUDAL_H")["series_id"].item()
subdaily = rr.pick(all_discharge, series_id=caudal_h_id)

    At automatic telemetric gauges, IDEAM derives daily maxima (Q_MX_D, NV_MX_D) and minima (Q_MN_D, NV_MN_D) from intra-hour sensor extremes, which can exceed the highest top-of-hour reading in CAUDAL_H or NIVEL_H on the same date. Because sub-daily averaging and extreme-value conventions vary between conventional and automatic equipment and are not stated per row in the exported CSV files, temporal_support remains unknown across all twenty series.

    Stage references, coordinates, and metadata

    River stage (nivel) is published in centimetres (cm) relative to the local staff-gauge zero (cero del limnímetro or cota cero in IDEAM's hydrometric manuals). RivRetrieve multiplies cm values by 0.01 to return metres (m), preserves "cm" in source_unit (with "m3/s" on discharge), and sets vertical_reference="gauge_zero" on all ten stage series. Genuine zero flows (0.0 m3/s), zero stages (0.0 m), and negative stages below local gauge zero are preserved without clamping.

    IDEAM's open-data CSV exports and station registry do not publish local gauge-zero elevations relative to sea level or dated histories of staff-gauge relocations. As a result, vertical_datum is unknown on all stage series. Do not add a station's general elevation (Altitud) to gauge_zero stage readings to estimate water-surface elevation above sea level.

    Station metadata (rr.metadata(selection)) exposes three fields from IDEAM's station catalogue (catalogo.csv):

    • Nombre as station_name
    • Corriente as water_body_name
    • Altitud as elevation (published as numeric text without an explicit unit or vertical datum in catalogo.csv, so elevation_unit and elevation_datum remain null in the packaged metadata)

    IDEAM's station catalogue does not publish catchment drainage areas, so drainage_area_* columns are null. Horizontal coordinates (Latitud and Longitud) are decimal degrees in Colombia, but because neither catalogo.csv nor its Socrata schema specifies whether coordinates refer to MAGNA-SIRGAS (EPSG:4686), WGS 84 (EPSG:4326), or unprojected legacy Observatorio de Bogotá surveys, crs is "unknown".

    Record currency and local caching

    IDEAM regenerates active S3 CSV files daily from AQUARIUS. Active telemetric gauges commonly extend to within one to three days of the current date, whereas conventional or decommissioned gauges end earlier. Each S3 request downloads the complete historical CSV file for one (station, series) pair (ranging from single-row 85-byte files up to roughly 35 MB on multi-decade hourly series).

    When cache="reuse" is passed to rr.fetch:

    • RivRetrieve caches the series over its observed [first_observation, last_observation] span. Subsequent queries whose requested window falls inside that span are served offline from the local store with zero network requests.
    • Requesting dates after last_observation (or before first_observation) triggers a fresh download of the S3 CSV file and updates any revised provisional values across the overlapping period. Because pre-record and post-record sub-spans are not marked complete in the local store, queries for future or newly published days are never blocked by stale empty-window cache entries.
    • If you want to pick up in-place recalculations of provisional values inside an already cached date span without extending the query end date, pass cache="refresh".

    When a requested S3 CSV object returns HTTP 404 (NoSuchKey), RivRetrieve reports a source.http_not_found warning in result.issues, leaves sibling stations and series unaffected, and retains any previously cached observations in the local store rather than erasing them during transient S3 export rebuilds.

    Time and data status

    Every S3 CSV row carries an unqualified wall-clock timestamp fechaObservacion formatted as YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS with no UTC offset. Outside 1992 and 1993, Colombia uses standard civil time UTC-05:00 year-round. Between May 1992 and February 1993, Colombia observed one hour of daylight saving time (Hora Gaviria, UTC-04:00 under Decreto 591 de 1992). IDEAM's AQUARIUS exporter formatted stored timestamps in historical local civil time without offsets:

    • During May 1992 through early February 1993, daily, monthly, and yearly rows carry 01:00:00 instead of 00:00:00, and conventional 06:00/18:00 readings shift to 07:00/19:00.
    • When clocks fell back on 1993-02-06 23:00:00, hourly series (CAUDAL_H, NIVEL_H) can contain two distinct consecutive measurements stamped with the identical wall-clock string 1993-02-06 23:00:00.

    Because an offset-free timestamp cannot disambiguate the folded 1993-02-06 23:00:00 hour and IDEAM's open documentation does not state the exact hydrological day boundary across historical conventional and automatic eras, RivRetrieve sets time_zone="unknown" and leaves day_definition, timestamp_anchor, and label_time unknown across all twenty series. rr.to_utc() refuses unknown time zones rather than guessing an offset on ambiguous wall-clock timestamps, and duplicate 1993-02-06 23:00:00 rows are preserved in source order.

    Date and timestamp clipping follows each series's time resolution:

    • Daily series (*_D): Clipped by calendar date (YYYY-MM-DD), so 1992-08-01 01:00:00 is matched as calendar date 1992-08-01 alongside ordinary 00:00:00 daily rows.
    • Sub-daily (CAUDAL_H, NIVEL_H), monthly (*_M), and yearly (*_A) series: Clipped by exact source timestamp. Monthly series are stamped on the first day of the month (YYYY-MM-01 00:00:00, or 01:00:00 during 1992-1993), and yearly series are stamped on January 1 (YYYY-01-01 00:00:00, or 1993-01-01 01:00:00); include those first-of-period dates in [start, end] when requesting monthly or yearly summaries.

    Each S3 CSV row includes a third column, nivelAprobacion, carrying IDEAM's AQUARIUS approval status:

    • Preliminar: provisional operational data (AQUARIUS approval level 900), subject to rating-curve and quality-control revision.
    • En revisión: data currently under hydrographic review (level 1100).
    • Definitivo: validated archival data (level 1200).
    • "" (empty string): legacy ungraded rows in some older series.

    Separate AQUARIUS Grade markers in IDEAM's open exports remain unpopulated (50 - INDEFINIDO). Following RivRetrieve's library-wide convention, rr.fetch returns observations across all nivelAprobacion states without filtering or modifying values. The nivelAprobacion value is preserved per row as a source annotation (schema co_ideam.s3, observation scope):

    result = rr.fetch(selection, start="2010-01-01", end="2010-01-07")

# Field catalogue
print(rr.annotations(result).select("schema", "name", "dtype").rows())
# [('co_ideam.s3', 'nivelAprobacion', 'String')]

# Append to the canonical frame
annotated = result.to_polars(annotations=True)
print(annotated.select("time", "value", "nivelAprobacion").head(3).rows())

    Missing dates and hours are simply absent from IDEAM's CSV files rather than written as placeholder rows.

Both rr.describe("co_ideam") and result.provenance expose license="Creative Commons Attribution | Share Alike 4.0 International" and citation="Instituto de Hidrología, Meteorología y Estudios Ambientales - IDEAM, Bogotá D.C.". For scientific reproducibility, also record the ten-digit station code, published series code (such as Q_MEDIA_D), requested period, and retrieval date.

    RivRetrieve paces requests to datos.ideam.gov.co at a maximum of one request per second. Use cache="reuse" when running repeated analyses across many stations or multi-decade records.
