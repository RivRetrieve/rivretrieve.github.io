    Retrieve one week of validated daily mean discharge at station 209486, Böheimkirchen on the Perschling:

    import rivretrieve as rr

selection = rr.find(
    provider="at_ehyd",
    station="209486",
    quantity="discharge",
    frequency="daily",
    statistic="mean",
)

result = rr.fetch(selection, start="2023-01-01", end="2023-01-07", cache="bypass")

preview = result.data.select("time", "time_zone", "value", "unit").head(3)
print(preview.write_csv(), end="")

print(result.data.height)
print(result.issues)
print(rr.series(result, annotations=True)["data_quality"].to_list())

2023-01-03T00:00:00.000000,unknown,0.109,m3/s

    The request returned seven published daily means in m³/s from eHYD's validated historical archive (owf_abfl_tagesmittel). The preview shows the first three days, and rr.series(result, annotations=True) shows the archive's Geprüfte Daten label from at_ehyd.series. time_zone is unknown because the exported CSV files do not state a UTC offset; see Time and data status. The empty tuple () means this retrieval reported no transport, parsing, or gap-marker issues. Attribution is still required when using the data; see Terms and citation.

    cache="bypass", the default for live rr.fetch calls, requests eHYD directly without reading or writing a local cache. Passing cache="reuse" stores the station's downloaded archive series locally so later historical queries for the same gauge and product reuse the cached series without downloading the full CSV file again. This output was checked live on 2026-11-03. Published values can change across hydrographic yearbook revisions. See Usage for selection options and result handling.

    Who measures, and who publishes

    Austria's Federal Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry, Climate and Environmental Protection, Regions and Water Management (Bundesministerium für Land- und Forstwirtschaft, Klima- und Umweltschutz, Regionen und Wasserwirtschaft, BMLUK), Department I/3 Water Balance (Wasserhaushalt, formerly the Hydrographic Central Office, HZB), publishes national hydrographic data through eHYD. Measurements are collected and validated in cooperation with the nine provincial hydrographic services (Hydrographische Landesdienste), the waterway operator viadonau on the Danube, and municipal water services. Appearance on eHYD does not mean that BMLUK operates every station itself.

    No API key or account is required. RivRetrieve searches the packaged station and series catalogue offline; rr.fetch then requests data directly from eHYD's public web services. Station identifiers are six-digit HZB numbers (hzbnr), kept as strings such as "209486".

    The packaged at_ehyd catalogue covers flowing surface waters only: 746 river gauges in EPSG:4326, of which 744 expose at least one selectable discharge, stage, or water temperature series (2 river gauges, 207480 and 230102, publish only sediment series). Standing-water lake gauges (Seepegel, 59 stations in eHYD's surface-water layer on lakes such as Bodensee, Neusiedler See, and Attersee), subsurface spring gauges (Quellen), groundwater wells, and precipitation or evaporation stations are excluded. Validated daily suspended sediment load (Schwebstoff-Tagesfracht) and daily bedload transport (Geschiebe-Tagesfracht) published at a subset of river gauges are also outside RivRetrieve's current quantity scope.

    What you can retrieve

    eHYD publishes river observations through two separate streams:

    1. Validated historical archive (Geprüfte Daten): Quality-checked CSV series reviewed for Austria's Hydrographic Yearbook (Hydrographisches Jahrbuch).
    2. Operational telemetry (Pegel Aktuell): Unchecked near-real-time sub-hourly telemetry (variant="data") and current-year daily comparison series (variant="current_value") published at telemetered gauges.

    A station may publish only a subset of these eleven series. Federal eHYD publishes validated river water temperature only as monthly means (owf_wassertemp_monatsmittel); daily and near-real-time river water temperature are not published on this federal service.

    In the validated archive, monthly minimum and maximum series report the lowest or highest instantaneous measurement recorded within each calendar month, stamped with its exact occurrence date and time, rather than the lowest or highest daily mean.

    RivRetrieve keeps validated archive series and operational telemetry strictly separate. It does not append unchecked telemetry onto validated yearbook records or calculate daily means from sub-hourly readings. Inspect all candidate series and station metadata at a gauge offline before fetching:

    all_discharge = rr.find(provider="at_ehyd", station="209486", quantity="discharge")
candidates = rr.series(all_discharge)
metadata = rr.metadata(all_discharge)

    Filtering with frequency="daily" and statistic="mean" selects only the validated daily-mean archive series (owf_abfl_tagesmittel) because the current-year daily comparison series (variant="current_value") has no established statistic and the sub-hourly telemetry series (variant="data") has no established frequency or statistic. Select either operational telemetry stream directly with variant:

    • Sub-hourly telemetry (Pegel Aktuell): rr.pick(all_discharge, variant="data") (or rr.pick(all_discharge, time_zone="UTC+01:00"))
    • Current-year daily comparison (Pegel Aktuell): rr.pick(all_discharge, variant="current_value")

    You can also pass a specific series_id from candidates to rr.pick(...) when targeting an individual series row.

    In sub-hourly telemetry (Pegel Aktuell, variant="data") responses, RivRetrieve reads only the data observation series and omits static warning-level lines (Niedrigwasser, Mittelwasser, and Hochwasser thresholds). In current-year daily comparison (Pegel Aktuell, variant="current_value") responses, RivRetrieve reads only the current calendar year's current_value series; it omits the prior-year overlay (last_year_value, which is aligned onto the current year's 365- or 366-day axis without its own calendar dates) and the 30-year climatological summary curves (min_data, max_data, mit_data, p5_data, p95_data).

    Stage references and moving gauge zeros

    River stage (Wasserstand) in both Geprüfte Daten and Pegel Aktuell is published in centimetres relative to the station's local gauge zero (Pegelnullpunkt). RivRetrieve multiplies cm values by 0.01 to return metres (m), keeps "cm" in source_unit, and sets vertical_reference="gauge_zero" on the series. Genuine water levels below gauge zero remain negative (for example down to -2.27 m at Danube station 207068, Linz), and published negative values in water-temperature exports (down to -2.8 °C in owf_wassertemp_monatsmittel at 200352, Unterhochsteg) are likewise preserved without clamping.

    Use care when interpreting multi-year stage records or comparing water levels across periods. At most Austrian river gauges (543 of 739 historical river gauges in the packaged snapshot, 537 with more than one distinct height), the local gauge zero has been reset or resurveyed over time. eHYD's station master sheets (Stammdaten) and stage CSV preambles list dated Pegelnullpunkt heights in metres above the Adriatic datum (m ü.A., Meter über Adria), without stating whether historical stage observations prior to a reset were adjusted to the latest datum or left relative to the datum active at the time of measurement.

    Because no single scalar elevation applies across a station's full history, the elevation_* columns are null in rr.metadata(selection) (state="no_metadata" in rr.metadata(selection, view="source")) and vertical_datum remains unknown on stage series. Do not add a single Pegelnullpunkt height to a long stage record without checking the station's dated datum table on eHYD. Where an eHYD stage response declares m ü.A or m ü.A. (Meter über Adria, water-surface elevation above sea level), RivRetrieve returns metres (m) without scaling, with vertical_reference="above_sea_level" and vertical_datum unknown; all river stage series in the packaged snapshot currently publish cm above gauge zero.

    Station metadata exposes station names (name from the historical registry and messstelle from Pegel Aktuell), water-body names (gewaesser and gewasser), and orographic catchment area (orogr.Einzugsgebiet [km²] with unit km² for gauges in the historical registry). When name and messstelle spell a gauge's name differently (51 overlapping gauges), summary station_name is null and both spellings remain visible in rr.metadata(selection, view="source"). Catchment areas retain eHYD's German decimal comma as JSON text, such as '"55,3"' at station 209486; see Station metadata for decoding details.

    Archive vintage and recent coverage

    The three series streams cover separate periods and leave an unpublished gap between the latest yearbook and the current calendar year:

    • Validated historical archive (Geprüfte Daten): Covers multi-decade histories up to the close of the latest published Hydrographic Yearbook. In the catalogue snapshot (2026-10-31) and live checks on 2026-11-03, validated river exports end at 2023-12-31 (marked by a closing boundary at 2024-01-01 00:00). Start years vary widely by gauge and quantity, and decommissioned gauges end earlier.
    • Current-year daily comparison (Pegel Aktuell, variant="current_value"): Covers only the current calendar year (2026 during 2026) up to the latest reported day. Trailing null padding for future dates later in the year is trimmed. The service ignores query parameters requesting earlier calendar years.
    • Near-real-time sub-hourly telemetry (Pegel Aktuell, variant="data"): Covers a rolling window of roughly seven to eight days at variable sub-hourly intervals (commonly 5, 10, 15, or 30 minutes, sometimes varying within a single week). Older readings age out as new telemetry arrives. Local caching (cache="reuse") accumulates readings already fetched across repeated runs, while readings that left the rolling window before being fetched cannot be recovered from this service.

    Because the validated archive ends at the latest published yearbook (2023) and the current-year daily comparison stream (variant="current_value") serves only the current calendar year, intermediate post-yearbook years (such as 2024 and 2025 before their yearbook release) are not yet available through federal ehyd.gv.at downloads. A validated archive request for dates after 2023-12-31 returns no rows for those unpublished years because the yearbook export has not yet reached them, rather than because no river measurements were taken.

    Time and data status

    Time-zone handling differs between sub-hourly telemetry and the daily or monthly series:

    • Sub-hourly telemetry (Pegel Aktuell, variant="data"): eHYD documents these timestamps as fixed Central European Time (Mitteleuropäische Zeit, MEZ, UTC+01:00 throughout the year without daylight-saving transitions). rr.fetch preserves the published MEZ wall-clock timestamps with time_zone="+01:00" (time_zone="UTC+01:00" in rr.series(...)), and rr.to_utc(result) shifts them to UTC (time_zone="+00:00"). Whether each sub-hourly value represents an instantaneous reading or a short interval average is not established.
    • Validated archive (Geprüfte Daten) and current-year daily comparison (Pegel Aktuell, variant="current_value"): Neither format states an explicit UTC offset in its machine-readable header or payload. Although BMLUK's Pegelordnung 2014 recommends MEZ (UTC+01:00), it also notes historical SOZ/MEZ summer-time practices, and older epochs in validated archive CSV files contain daily timestamps at 07:00 or 01:00 before shifting to 00:00 (at historical manual staff gauges, Lattenpegel, a scheduled morning reading counted as the daily value). RivRetrieve therefore keeps time_zone="unknown" and leaves day_definition unknown on both streams; rr.to_utc() refuses unknown time zones rather than assuming UTC+01:00 across all historical years.

    On validated interval-mean archive series (owf_abfl_tagesmittel, owf_wasserst_tagesmittel, and owf_wassertemp_monatsmittel), each timestamp marks the start of its validity interval (timestamp_anchor="start"), in accordance with the CSV preamble rule ("Der Intervallwert gilt bis zum nächsten Zeitpunkt mit einem Wert oder Lücke"). At Danube station 207340 (Wilhering), the W-Monatsminima CSV file declares and contains daily (T,1) stage minima rather than monthly minima; RivRetrieve withholds frequency="monthly" on that station's owf_wasserst_monatsminima series so monthly filters do not return daily rows.

    eHYD labels its historical CSV downloads Geprüfte Daten (checked data) and displays "ungeprüfte Rohdaten!" (unchecked raw data) on Pegel Aktuell exports, noting that live telemetry can contain sensor faults, ice or weed effects, backwater, or transmission gaps. Neither stream carries per-observation quality flags. RivRetrieve attaches the verbatim stream label in the series-grain annotation schema at_ehyd.series (scope="series", field data_quality of type String) without making a quality judgement of its own. Use rr.annotations(result) to inspect attached metadata and rr.series(result, annotations=True) to join data_quality to the series table; see Usage.

    Keep missing-data representations distinct when inspecting results:

    • In validated archive (Geprüfte Daten) CSV files, missing spans are encoded as timestamped Lücke (gap) transition lines rather than one null row per missing day, and interval files end with a closing Lücke boundary line at the end of the export window. RivRetrieve omits Lücke lines without fabricating rows, suppresses the closing-boundary marker, and records an informational source.gap_marker issue in result.issues when interior gaps occur.
    • In Pegel Aktuell telemetry responses (variant="data" and variant="current_value"), interior missing entries published as null remain rows with value=null.
    • When a Pegel Aktuell gauge is currently offline and returns an empty response, rr.fetch returns no rows (and emits a source.inventory_unresolved warning in result.issues when that telemetry series was requested explicitly). Empty or malformed validated archive files report an error or warning in result.issues; check issues before interpreting an empty result.

    Terms and citation

    Official INSPIRE Austria metadata records for the hydrographic surface-water station network and the current hydrographic measurement service state that the data are licensed for private and commercial reuse free of charge under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), with mandatory source attribution:

    Datenquelle: ehyd.gv.at

    In German, the INSPIRE attribution clause requires "Datenquelle: ehyd.gv.at", wobei "ehyd.gv.at" als Link auf https://ehyd.gv.at auszuführen ist (linking ehyd.gv.at to https://ehyd.gv.at). Both rr.describe("at_ehyd") and result.provenance expose license="CC BY 4.0" and citation="Datenquelle: ehyd.gv.at". For scientific traceability, also record the station HZB number, product, requested period, and retrieval date.

    The eHYD portal notes that data are published pursuant to § 55o (2) Wasserrechtsgesetz and § 4 (1)(2) Umweltinformationsgesetz without warranty for completeness, continuity, or accuracy, and prohibits automated extraction outside content expressly offered for download (both per-station Geprüfte Daten CSV files and Pegel Aktuell chart CSV exports are offered for download on the portal). An older 2022 general user guide (page 14) also contains an own-use and no-paid-resale statement alongside the CC BY 4.0 INSPIRE licence declarations. RivRetrieve paces live requests politely (at most one request per second) and caches downloaded archive series locally when cache="reuse" is passed.
