    Retrieve published daily discharge at station 212600, Huayabamba on the Río Huayabamba:

    import rivretrieve as rr

selection = rr.find(
    provider="pe_senamhi",
    station="212600",
    quantity="discharge",
    frequency="daily",
)
selection = rr.pick(selection, variant="current")

result = rr.fetch(selection, start="2024-01-01", end="2024-01-07", cache="bypass")

preview = result.data.select("time", "time_zone", "value", "unit").head(3)
print(preview.write_csv(), end="")

print(result.data.height)
print([(issue.severity, issue.code) for issue in result.issues])

    Output:

    time,time_zone,value,unit
2024-01-01T00:00:00.000000,unknown,1333.9661,m3/s
2024-01-02T00:00:00.000000,unknown,1184.2294,m3/s
2024-01-03T00:00:00.000000,unknown,1306.6894,m3/s
7
[('info', 'provenance.license_not_established'), ('info', 'provenance.citation_not_established')]

    The request returned seven daily discharge values in m³/s for 1 to 7 January 2024. Both endpoint dates are included. The preview shows the first three values. The series requested is SENAMHI's published current field. RivRetrieve reads the daily values SENAMHI publishes; it does not calculate them from more frequent observations. RivRetrieve has not established whether current represents a daily mean, nor when its observation day begins and ends. RivRetrieve preserves these physical unknowns and returns time_zone="unknown". Do not assume the daily values represent midnight to midnight UTC or local time.

    The two informational issues report that RivRetrieve has not established an official open licence or citation template for SENAMHI. They are not retrieval failures; see Terms and citation.

    cache="bypass" requests the SENAMHI API directly rather than a local cache. This output was checked on 2026-11-01. Published values can change, so a later retrieval need not reproduce this output exactly. See Usage for general selection and result handling.

    Who measures, and who publishes

    SENAMHI operates Peru's national hydrological observing network across the Pacific, Amazon, and Lake Titicaca drainage basins. It measures river stage and discharge and publishes them through the Observatorio Nacional del Tiempo, Agua y Clima del Perú. RivRetrieve reads the Observatorio's public backend API at servicios.senamhi.gob.pe/api.

    The network includes conventional hydrological stations (categories HLM and HLG), where observers record water levels at scheduled hours, and automatic telemetry stations (categories EHA and EHMA), which transmit hourly sensor readings.

    Of the 771 station identities retained in RivRetrieve's packaged catalogue from SENAMHI's station registry, 270 are hydrological stations with supported series in this snapshot.

    Station identifiers are SENAMHI's station codes, such as 212600 or 47251282.

    What you can retrieve

    RivRetrieve provides river discharge and stage across two SENAMHI subsystems: the PHISIS hydrological monitoring service and the surface observational station archive.

    PHISIS hydrological monitoring

    PHISIS publishes discharge and water level under the /api/hydrology/* routes:

    Quantity filter Source field / variant Frequency Statistic Source unit Returned unit
    discharge current Daily Unknown m³/s m³/s
    discharge minimum Daily Unknown m³/s m³/s
    discharge maximum Daily Unknown m³/s m³/s
    discharge value Hourly Unknown m³/s m³/s
    stage current Daily Unknown m, m.s.n.m m
    stage minimum Daily Unknown m, m.s.n.m m
    stage maximum Daily Unknown m, m.s.n.m m
    stage value Hourly Unknown m, m.s.n.m m

    These field names are SENAMHI's published PHISIS values, returned in published_id. Stage has two separate series per field: one in metres and one above sea level in m.s.n.m (see Units and vertical reference). Narrow to one field with rr.pick(selection, variant=...) using the value shown above, or select an individual series directly with series_id from rr.series(selection).

    Each daily PHISIS response contains three separate published fields: current, minimum, and maximum. RivRetrieve preserves each field as a distinct series. PHISIS distinguishes daily date coordinates from hourly timestamp coordinates. These establish frequency="daily" and frequency="hourly", independently of statistic. RivRetrieve has not established whether current or hourly value is a mean or an instantaneous reading, or which period and population the daily minimum and maximum summarize. Their statistics remain unknown.

    Daily queries select whole published dates, including when the request bounds contain clock times. This treats dates as source coordinates; it does not establish the observation day's boundaries, temporal support or time zone. A daily frequency filter matches the daily series, but a mean statistic filter does not.

    Selecting quantity="discharge" or quantity="stage" without specifying a series_id selects every supported series for that quantity at the station. Use rr.series(selection) to inspect the available series IDs, or select an individual series directly with series_id.

    Station archive readings

    The station archive publishes water levels in metres (m) under /api/station/stations/*:

    Quantity filter Source field / variant Frequency Statistic Source unit Returned unit
    stage NIVEL_DEL_RIO_06H Unknown Unknown m m
    stage NIVEL_DEL_RIO_10H Unknown Unknown m m
    stage NIVEL_DEL_RIO_14H Unknown Unknown m m
    stage NIVEL_DEL_RIO_18H Unknown Unknown m m
    stage NIVEL_DEL_RIO_PROMEDIO Unknown Unknown m m
    stage N_NIVELAGUA Unknown Unknown m m
    stage N_NIVELMEDIO Unknown Unknown m m
    stage N_NIV_INST_00 Unknown Unknown m m
    stage N_NIV_INST_10 Unknown Unknown m m

    These field names are SENAMHI's published monthly station-archive JSON keys, also returned in published_id. Narrow to one field with rr.pick(selection, variant=...) using the value shown above, or select an individual series directly with series_id from rr.series(selection).

    These nine fields remain distinct products in RivRetrieve. Frequency and statistic remain unestablished because the available evidence does not define their meanings across the supported fields and historical records. This is a gap in the evidence, not an explicit statement by SENAMHI that the meanings are unspecified.

    The 06H, 10H, 14H and 18H labels name clock hours, not sampling intervals. The hydrometry manual describes scheduled readings and an arithmetic average, but their applicability to NIVEL_DEL_RIO_PROMEDIO and individual archive records is not established. The N_NIV* suffixes do not establish clock times or averaging methods. Monthly request partitions do not establish observation frequency.

    Units and vertical reference

    Discharge is published in m³/s and requires no conversion. Stage is published in metres (m) or metres above sea level (m.s.n.m). Both return values in metres (m).

    When the source unit is m.s.n.m, RivRetrieve marks vertical_reference="above_sea_level". SENAMHI does not name a geodetic vertical datum, so vertical_datum remains null. For relative stage in metres, vertical_reference is null.

    How far back, and how recent

    Record periods vary by station. PHISIS provides recent monitoring and historical series. Daily PHISIS requests are partitioned by hydrological year (starting 1 September), while hourly requests use a two-day backward span. Station archive requests query individual calendar months.

    A station being listed does not guarantee data for every quantity or period. When SENAMHI has no observations for a requested interval, RivRetrieve returns no rows. Published null values remain rows with value=null. Absent dates supply no rows. Failed source responses remain issues reported alongside any successful records.

    SENAMHI records occasionally contain negative values or future-dated timestamps; RivRetrieve returns these values as published.

    Time and data status

    SENAMHI timestamps and date labels carry no explicit time zone offset. RivRetrieve sets time_zone="unknown". Do not assume timestamps are UTC or Peru local time (UTC-5).

    PHISIS DAILY current is not established as a daily mean, and its averaging day definition is unknown.

    PHISIS automatic-station views on the Observatorio display the notice "Esta información corresponde a datos crudos registrados por la estación automática" ("This information corresponds to raw data recorded by the automatic station"). This applies to PHISIS automatic-station series; it does not generalize to the conventional station-archive series above.

    Neither PHISIS JSON payloads nor conventional station-archive HTML tables publish observation-level or series-level quality flags, so rr.annotations(result) is empty for pe_senamhi. SENAMHI provides data without warranty of fitness for a particular purpose. RivRetrieve makes no quality judgements of its own.

    Rate limits

    SENAMHI requests must respect an access limit of at most one request per second. RivRetrieve's transport applies appropriate pacing. Avoid running concurrent unthrottled retrievals against SENAMHI endpoints.

    Direct connections to servicios.senamhi.gob.pe from some cloud or datacenter IP ranges can time out even when the same requests succeed from residential or institutional networks. If a retrieval times out on a cloud virtual machine or a CI runner, this network boundary is a likely cause, not a problem with the request.

    Terms and citation

    SENAMHI's service terms state:

    Información recopilada y trabajada por el Servicio Nacional de Meteorología e Hidrología del Perú. El uso que se le da a esta información es de mi (nuestra) entera responsabilidad.

    In English, unofficially: information compiled and processed by SENAMHI; the use made of this information is the user's sole responsibility.

    SENAMHI does not declare a standardized open licence code or citation template. RivRetrieve flags these conditions with informational issues (provenance.license_not_established and provenance.citation_not_established). When using these data, cite SENAMHI as the source and retain station identifiers, quantities, and retrieval dates for traceability.
